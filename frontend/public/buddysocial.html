OUR HISTORY

Why we started The Buddy Social: Navigating Ireland's tedious career process for grads — from resumes to real jobs — shouldn't be a lonely journey. Founded in 2024, we've been creating cosy spaces where students and graduates can connect, learn, and grow together. Our community has helped hundreds of young professionals find their footing in Dublin's competitive job market.
