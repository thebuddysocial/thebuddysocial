OUR HISTORY

Why we started The Buddy Social: Navigating Ireland's tedious career process for grads — from resumes to real jobs — shouldn't be lonely. Founded in 2025, we create cosy Dublin spaces where students connect, learn, and grow. Our community helps hundreds find footing in competitive markets.
